# TSA Worklog Discord Bot

A Discord bot designed to streamline the process of managing worklogs for [TSA (Technology Student Association)](https://tsaweb.org/) competitions, specifically made for the [North Creek High School](https://northcreek.nsd.org/) community. This bot integrates seamlessly with Google Docs to automatically create, update, and manage worklog documents for your teams... all from within Discord. 

There's also some utility commands such as searching for all the competitors for your event within the school, etc. 

## Features

- **Within Discord:** Every feature is accessed through just Discord-- talk to your team while working without switching tabs!
- **Integrated to You:** You don't have to invite it to a server to use it! Just authorize the bot, and you can use it anywhere!
- **Worklogs Management:** Allows you to manage all of your worklog. Yes, this supports MULTIPLE worklogs!
- **Seamless Sharing:** Automatically shares your worklog with all of your teammates and your other emails
- **Easy Entry Lookups/Edits:** Allows you to view individual or a batch of entries and edit them from just within Discord. Obviously, you can add new entries as well!
- **Export to PDF:** You don't even have to leave Discord to export your worklog to a PDF!
- **Utility Features:** Includes many utility features, such as searching up all the events that someone is in, all the teams in a given event, or someone's past competition history. **Limited to NCHS members*

> ...With this bot, you will never have to open your worklog document!
